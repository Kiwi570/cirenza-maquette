      01Fall 2026, issue XIV · “Finding Light: Encaustic Expression of Hope”

      Beyond the Surface

      The Luminous Layer: Cultivating Hope through Creativity in an Uncertain World

      To plant sunflower seeds in alpine soil, collaborate with unpredictable wax, or open a studio door to a community is a quiet, radical act of faith.

      Art is my vehicle for cultivating connections; it was never my end goal. I needed agency, a way to add more positive value to the world. Which led me to found Espace Créa-Tea-Vité in La Clusaz, acquiring a former mountain restaurant to realize a creative physical sanctuary for those overwhelmed by modern demands.

      In the feature: Emergence, Seaside Village and the Pink Flowers, Stand Tall and Face the Light and Rose Coloured Glasses, with their materials and processes.
