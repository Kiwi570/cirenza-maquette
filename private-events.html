A unique artistic venue for private events, cultural gatherings, and creative experiences in La Clusaz

Located within an intimate contemporary gallery, Espace Créa-Tea-Vité offers a warm, inspiring environment designed for meaningful connection, creativity, and memorable moments. Whether you are planning a conference, private dinner, cultural event, or team-building experience, the space blends artistic atmosphere with practical flexibility.

01The venue

  • A separate apéro / reception area distinct from the main gallery space
  • Fully equipped kitchen: plates, dishes, glassware, and serving essentials included
  • Small lit stage area also available

The gallery setting creates an intimate yet professional ambiance — ideal for gatherings that go beyond a traditional event space.

02Private creative experiences

In addition to venue rental, guests may book a private creative event led by the resident artist. These sessions are designed to spark imagination, encourage collaboration, and provide a memorable shared experience.

  • Guided artistic workshops
  • Creative thinking sessions
  • Custom cultural experiences tailored to your group

Espace Créa-Tea-Vité specializes in creative workshops that combine artistic practice with innovative thinking. These experiences are ideal for offsites, leadership retreats, and team-building programs. The artist behind the space has led talks, workshops, and creative sessions for international institutions and organizations including Barclays, Stanford University, Apple, major financial institutions, and public and private cultural groups.
