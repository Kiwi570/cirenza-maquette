      02Lisa Cirenza · Fine Art · archive line

      Collectors' editions

      Small 15 × 20 cm works mounted in thick acrylic blocks, from my archive of digital work. They sell quickly (one went within fifteen minutes of the Salon d'Automne opening) and bring new collectors to my work.

      Each piece is dated twice: the year of the image and the year of the block (for example, “Tube series, 2017 — acrylic block, 2026”). Bought directly online, with a short note on format, signature and whether each piece is unique or editioned.
