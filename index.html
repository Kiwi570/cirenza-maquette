      Atelier · the worksEspace Créa-Tea-Vité

      Scroll down through the strata

      01Reading time in the layers

      I was trained to read time in glacial and rock layers. Now I'm interpreting the latest layer, the Anthropocene, in paint and wax.

      In a melting world, I paint hope as light.

      When we truly immerse ourselves in nature, the boundary between self and world dissolves. We sense a profound unity, an indivisible thread weaving us into the living fabric around us.

      02The Luminous Layer

      Beneath the surface

      We live in an age of profound paradox. We are more hyper-connected than at any point in human history, yet that very connectivity can isolate us into algorithm-directed narratives.

      My work is an intentional antidote to this surface-level consumption. It refuses to deliver its message in a fleeting glance. Instead, it rewards the patient viewer: the longer you look, the more you discover, and the more questions you begin to ask about what lies beneath.

      — Lisa Cirenza, Wax Fusion, Fall 2026

      07Espace · gallery · tea room · studio

      Espace Créa-Tea-Vité

      A refuge* for genuine conviviality, warm drinks, and quiet inspiration. Step inside to slow down and recharge—whether you are here to savor a signature latte, take in the latest gallery exhibition, or pick up fine art supplies and gifts. Come to create, or simply sit back and enjoy a real break.

      In 2025, Lisa turned the former L'Ourson restaurant into a studio, tea room, gallery and creative space. She designed the renovation herself, drawing on her years in Japan to bring wood, textures, colours and nature into conversation.
