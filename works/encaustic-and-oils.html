Encaustic and Oils

Cool Reflections and other works in this series

Encaustic is the process of painting with hot wax. It dates back to the ancient Greeks who taught the Egyptians, who then used it on their mummies. Adding resin to the wax increases the durability and the melt temperature, so these are archival works, destined to last.
