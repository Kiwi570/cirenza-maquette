The London Tube Series

The London Tube is a veritable microcosm of differences in culture, socioeconomic backgrounds, religions, languages--and yet--everyone is sharing a purpose (to get from one point to another), and often an armrest with often unlikely partners. This series explores the incongruous nature of these connections, and the hope that lies therein.
