      01Your creative coach

      Lisa Cirenza

      Artist, Speaker, Mentor

      I’m the artist who will happily compare your painting to a roast chicken. Not taking ourselves too seriously is fundamental to everything I do: laughter loosens the hand, quiets the inner critic, and makes room for courageous creativity.

      I’ve led workshops for Stanford University, Apple, Adobe and Barclays, and for artists, entrepreneurs and innovators around the world, even the wee little ones. I’m a “Painting with Fire” instructor, and I’ve guided treks on the glaciers of Alaska and in the mountains.

      I’ve worked with refugees, homeless mothers, burned-out founders, recent retirees, and emerging and professional artists. The goal is always the same: tapping into our creative potential through seeing, slowing down and creating.

      My work has been exhibited at the Grand Palais in Paris, in Venice during the Biennale, at the Asian Art Biennale in Dhaka, at the Kyoto Museum of Art, in Miami during Art Basel week, and in London, New York and Silicon Valley. It’s held in institutional and private collections in Europe, North America and Asia.

      Wherever we begin, I’ll help you turn challenges into opportunities, follow your curiosity, and leave with a recipe that’s entirely your own.
