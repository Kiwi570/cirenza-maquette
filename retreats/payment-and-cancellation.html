      Retreat Payment Schedule and Cancellation Policy

      Think of this as the recipe card for the practical side: short, clear and no hidden ingredients. If anything is unclear, just write to me. I’d much rather answer a question than have you wonder.

      1. Securing your place

      A deposit of €200 secures your place on the retreat. It is deducted from your first payment.

      2. Payment schedule

      50% of the retreat fee is due six months before the retreat begins. The remaining 50% is due three months before the retreat begins. We’ll send you each payment request by email in good time. Any early-bird, returner or other reduction is taken off your final payment.

      3. Right of withdrawal

      In accordance with Article L221-28 of the French Consumer Code, the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to accommodation and leisure services provided on a specific date or for a specific period. Your booking is therefore final once your deposit is paid.

      4. If you cancel

      Our retreats are small and planned around each group, so most of our costs (lodging, materials and excursions) are committed well in advance. Please let us know in writing (by email) if you need to cancel. The following cancellation fees apply: More than six months before the retreat: the €200 deposit is retained. Between six and three months before the retreat: 50% of the retreat fee is retained. Less than three months before the retreat: 100% of the retreat fee is retained. If you cancel, you won’t be asked for any payment that isn’t yet due.

      5. Transferring your place

      You may transfer your place to another person who meets the conditions of the retreat, provided you let us know in writing at least seven days before the retreat begins. If we find a replacement from our waiting list, the same applies. In either case, we’ll refund everything you’ve paid, less the actual costs of the transfer, up to a maximum of 5% of the amount paid. You and the person taking your place remain jointly responsible for any balance due and for these costs.

      6. Exceptional circumstances

      If exceptional and unavoidable circumstances at or near the retreat location (for example a natural disaster or a public health emergency) significantly affect the retreat or your travel to it, you may cancel without any cancellation fee and receive a full refund.

      7. If we cancel

      If we have to cancel the retreat for any reason, you’ll receive a full refund of everything you’ve paid, within 14 days.

      8. Travel insurance

      We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance that covers cancellation. Past guests who had to cancel were very glad to have been fully insured.

      Questions?

      Write to me at Atelier@cirenza.com

      Pack your curiosity and a sense of humour; I’ll handle the small print.
