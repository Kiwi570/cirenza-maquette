    01From L'Ourson to Créa-Tea-Vité

    A refuge, by design

    In 2025, Lisa turned the former L'Ourson restaurant into a studio, tea room, gallery and creative space. She designed the renovation herself, drawing on her years in Japan to bring wood, textures, colours and nature into conversation.

    Art is my vehicle for cultivating connections; it was never my end goal. I needed agency, a way to add more positive value to the world. Which led me to found Espace Créa-Tea-Vité in La Clusaz, acquiring a former mountain restaurant to realize a creative physical sanctuary for those overwhelmed by modern demands.

    — Lisa Cirenza, Wax Fusion, 2026
    • 🔔A small bell brought back from Nepal rings at the start of every class, inviting participants to let go of their preconceptions and reconnect with their creative potential.
