04Current exhibition

On now July – October 2026

La Gravité de la Grâce

Gravité de la grâce captures the beautiful tension between weight and weightlessness. This exhibition unites two local artists who find soaring joy in seemingly opposing realms: BNT Designs, through fluid metal sculptures of local flight and motion, and Lisa Cirenza, through vibrant visual celebrations of human resilience in Cuba. Together, these metals, pigments, and pixels reveal grace as an active agent of transformation—proving that lightness can be found in the heaviest of mediums and circumstances.

BNT Designs

BNT Designs transforms heavy, rigid industrial metal into fluid, weightless expressions of motion. Focusing on dynamic local subjects—from paragliders and cyclists to soaring montgolfières—these sculpted steel works actively defy physical gravity. By trapping high-energy movement in iron and steel, BNT Designs captures the fleeting, soaring grace of joy found in pure action and continuous transformation.

Lisa Cirenza

Lisa Cirenza works across materials—waxes, pigments, aluminium, acrylics, inks, rice papers—building and breaking down images to reveal histories embedded within matter itself. Through this deeply tactile process, her work investigates an indomitable joy that persists despite the complex socio-political circumstances in Cuba. By layering texture and color, Cirenza transforms emotional and historical gravity into a vibrant visual celebration of human resilience and active grace.
