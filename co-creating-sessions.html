      Co-Creating Sessions

      A place to work on, and get advice on your personal creative projects ...

      Come paint, draw, write... Sip a warm drink (included) and enjoy personalised guidance from the artist to help refine your next spark of inspiration. Bring your own materials, use some of our basics, or buy one of our kits. If you don't have a specific project, be inspired by our library of books and images. Water, table easels, and other accessories will be available to borrow.

      Cancellation Policy

      No refunds are available once the session has started. Any cancellation request must be submitted in writing to atelier@cirenza.com, at least 72 hours before the course begins. If your space can be filled after cancellation, 90% of your total payment will be refunded. If the space cannot be filled, the fee is non-refundable.

      Conditions d’annulation

      Aucun remboursement n’est possible après le début du cours, ni en cas d’absence à une séance. Toute demande d’annulation doit être adressée par écrit à atelier@cirenza.com, au moins 72 heures avant le début du cours. Si votre place peut être comblée après une annulation, 90 % du montant total payé vous seront remboursés. Dans le cas contraire, les frais restent dus et ne sont pas remboursables.
