      Private bespoke courses

      For hotels, concierges and event planners: private, bespoke art sessions for your guests at Espace Créa-Tea-Vité, in La Clusaz.

      I don’t teach you how to paint a perfect flower; I teach you how to see and to express the garden.

      01Private Atelier Sessions

      Focus on process and medium

      • 01

        Wax & Mixed Media

        Exploration through texture, encaustic, and layered discovery.

      • 02

        Wax Monoprinting

        Immediate, intuitive mark-making through the fluidity of wax.

      • 03

        The Artist’s Eye

        Moving beyond representation to truly see—the fundamental skill of visual intelligence.

      • 04

        Abstraction & The Creative Voice

        Breaking the habit of "doing it right" to find your authentic aesthetic language.

      • 05

        Digital Mastery (iPad Art)

        Advanced digital workflows. Instruction refined through partnerships with Apple, Adobe, and Stanford University.
