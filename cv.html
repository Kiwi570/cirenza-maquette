      CV

      Lisa Cirenza is a painter based in the French Alps whose work explores connection, and what lies beneath the surface of what we think we see.

      Education and research

      • Stanford University: Applied Earth Sciences, including studies at the École des Mines de Paris; co-terminal master's in International Policy Studies
      • National Science Foundation scholar, Juneau Icefield Research Program: field researcher on glaciological expeditions, Alaska and British Columbia
      • Art training: Royal Drawing School, London; Central Saint Martins, London; Norfolk Painting School (diploma); old masters' mediums and techniques at the atelier of Jean-Pierre Braz, Paris; sumi ink under a master in Japan

      Collections

      • Stanford Hospital Art Collection, Palo Alto, California (permanent collection, 2019)
      • Nepal Academy of Fine Arts, Kathmandu
      • Michael Tubbs, former Mayor of Stockton, California
      • Private collections in the UK, USA, France, Finland and Japan, including founders and partners of leading investment and family-owned groups

      Press

      • Rachel Spence, “London's best satellite fairs: ‘different conversations and collegiate feel’”, Financial Times, October 2017. Pull quote: “…blends tradition and contemporary practice with authority.”
      • BBC documentary on Stockton featuring Dream Big (title and year to follow)
      • Maisons et Jardins, feature article, 2021

      Publications

      • The Power of Ice (Chicago Children's Press), co-authored as Lisa Gitkin
