      Upcoming exhibition

      La Gravité de la Grâce

      Opens 3 December 2026 December 2026 – April 2027 · Espace Créa-Tea-Vité, La Clusaz

      La Gravité de la Grâce explores the beautiful tension between weight and weightlessness. This winter the conversation deepens. Lisa Cirenza’s new series meets the photographs of Le Petit Lapin, an anonymous presence whose mountain landscapes are shaped by time, accumulation and erasure. BNT Designs’ steel sculptures of flight and motion stay on from the summer. Together, metal, pigment, wax and light show that grace can be found in the heaviest of materials and circumstances.

      01The artists

      BNT Designs

      BNT Designs transforms heavy, rigid industrial metal into fluid, weightless expressions of motion. Focusing on dynamic local subjects—from paragliders and cyclists to soaring montgolfières—these sculpted steel works actively defy physical gravity. By trapping high-energy movement in iron and steel, BNT Designs captures the fleeting, soaring grace of joy found in pure action and continuous transformation.

      Lisa Cirenza

      Lisa Cirenza works across waxes, pigments, watercolours, acrylics, inks and rice papers. Her new body of work emerges from four forces: light, rock, water and time. An earth scientist by training, she paints the way landscapes are made: adding and subtracting, layer after layer, in an iterative process that mirrors the land itself. Le Petit Lapin lets nature create the forms through time and patience; Cirenza uses her tools to recreate those forces in the studio, compressing and excavating centuries in layers of wax and pigment.

      A Record of Time

      Le Petit Lapin

      Le Petit Lapin

      Le Petit Lapin is not a person, but a presence. Anonymous and deliberately absent from public view, he lets his images speak for themselves. His photographs don’t document; they observe. They bear witness to landscapes shaped by time, accumulation and erasure, where something remains without ever imposing itself. True to his nature, Le Petit Lapin will be observing from a distance.
